Preventing Dental Emergencies

The best emergency is the one that never happens. Everyone has different dental needs, but a few habits help nearly anyone enjoy better oral health. Visit your regular dentist at least twice a year for checkups and cleanings. Regular exams help you track your dental health and catch problems before they escalate into painful emergencies.

Brush at least twice a day or after every meal, floss daily and use a dental rinse to prevent gum disease and plaque buildup. If you play contact sports or enjoy activities that carry any risk of injury to the mouth, invest in a high quality mouthguard to protect your teeth and gums from trauma.

Of course, accidents still happen. When they do, our emergency dentist is only a phone call away.
